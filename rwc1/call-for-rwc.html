Our Approach

How We Diagnose

The best mechanics don't guess — they follow a logical process. Here's how we find the real fault.

1

Understand & list causes

We don't guess. Every job starts by understanding how your vehicle's systems work, then building a list of the likely causes for the fault.

2

Eliminate, one by one

Starting with the most likely cause — and the simplest, most cost-effective checks first — we work down the list until the real problem is found.

3

Find the root cause

You're not paying to replace parts on a hunch. We gather evidence and rule out possibilities until only the actual fault remains.
